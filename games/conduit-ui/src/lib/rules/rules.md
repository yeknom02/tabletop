# Conduit

## by Dan Domme Rules of Play v. 0.8b, November 22, 2026

## Overview

## _Players are private investors building a regional power grid and seeking to provide power to all customers. The player who contributes most to the grid will earn the most cash and win the game._

## Game Components

- 64 multi-use station tiles (square)
- 1 Auction Board
- Multiple red sticks (route blocking markers)
- 1 Central Station tile (blocked connections on all sides)
- 75 wooden cubes in each player color (i.e., 15 per player)
- 1 Start Player marker
- These rules

_Note: play money is not provided. It is suggested to use poker chips or similar to track cash._

## Setup

Divide the tiles into stacks by letter, shuffling each separately and placing them face-down. Place the Central Station tile in the center, as well as one tile each from stacks A–D to make a simple cross pattern: A to the North, B to the East, C to the South, and D to the West. Terminal orientations (black/red) should be random.

Give each player all the cubes in their player color, as well as \$5. Each player begins with 5 available cubes in supply, with the remaining 10 kept in a reserve.

## Order of Play

Each round will proceed as follows:

1. Fill the tile market
2. Place bids
3. Resolve bids
4. Pay Revenue
5. Infrastructure Loss

After each round, the Start Player marker moves to the next player clockwise.

1. ## Fill the Tile Market

The auction board has up to six spaces for tiles, each with its own bidding track. In alphabetical order, flip the top tile of each stack over to build a market of tiles that is one greater than the number of players. Order is circulating, i.e., the A stack follows the D stack. Once the market is full, take note of the final tile, because the market will be filled starting with the next tile in order for the following round.

_Ex: In a 4-player game, the first market will consist of tiles \[A, B, C, D, A\], followed by \[B, C, D, A, B\] in the following round, etc._

The station tiles feature a tile identification letter (A–D) and a section of the rectangular power grid network, with one branch connected to each tile edge. Each branch features one of two terminal colors: positive (red) or negative (black).

2. ## Place Bids

Beginning with the Start Player, each player in turn takes a single cube from their current available supply and places one on the lowest available space of a bidding track of their choice. Biding continues until the following conditions are both met: (1) at least 3 cubes have been placed by every player, and (2) all tiles have at least one bid. Passing is not allowed.

3. ## Resolve Bids

Resolve tiles in order from left to right in the following manner. For each bid track, the player with the bottom-most cube on the track has the option of purchasing the tile for a price equal to the total number of cubes currently in the stack.

If they purchase the tile, they pay \$1 to the owner of each other cube on the map, with each of their own cubes resulting in \$1 paid to the bank instead. Any cubes from other players are returned to their owners’ available supply along with their payment.

If the player chooses not to pay for the tile, then they remove their lowest cube to their available supply and take \$1 from the bank. The choice then passes to the next player above them on the bidding track.

If all players pass on the tile, the current Start Player chooses how to add it to the tile to the network, as follows, albeit face down and therefore with only open connections.

To add a tile to the network, it must be placed according to the following rules:

- If it is possible to place the tile such that it connects to more than one other tile, you must do so.

- If two connectors are of a similar color, or at least one of the tiles is face down, their connection is considered “open.” If the connectors are of opposite colors, the connection is considered “closed.” Place a blocking marker on each closed connection. You must place your tile such that you create at least one valid connection.

Finally, the player winning the bid takes their winning cube and places it on their tile, as well as any additional cubes from their available supply that they wish. They may not place any cubes from their reserve or bidding tracks which are not yet resolved.

Continue resolving bids in this manner until all are resolved.

4. ## Calculate and Pay Revenue

For each Power Station on the board:

1. The owning player traces the shortest possible non-branching route from their Power Station to another station of the next letter. This route cannot traverse closed connections or the Central Station.
2. The player earns \$1, plus \$1 per station on the route.
3. Remove one cube from the tile, placing it in the matching player’s reserve.

## 5\. Infrastructure Loss

After all income has been distributed, each station on the board which is devoid of cubes is flipped to its reverse side, except for the original four stations bordering the Central Station. All connections with this tile are now open.

Unless it is the end of the fifth round, all players now take two additional cubes from their reserve into their supply.

## End of the Game

The game ends after the fifth round. If any cubes remain on the board, repeat the calculation of revenue and infrastructure loss steps until no cubes remain on the board.

The player with the most cash on hand wins. Ties are broken in favor of the player with the most remaining cubes in their available supply. In the case that there is still a tie, all tied players must share the victory.
