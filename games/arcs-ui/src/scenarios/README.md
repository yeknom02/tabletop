# Arcs harness scenarios

Written by `scripts/build-scenarios.mjs` (build the logic package first). Each is a 3-player game
without leaders or lore, with the first player taking actions after leading a card.

## Battle ready

The first player led Aggression 3. In the first cluster in play they have 4 ships at the arrow
planet, where the second player has 2 ships and a city; 2 ships at the gate, with the third
player's fresh and damaged ship; and the second player has a starport and a ship at the hex planet.
The second player holds Fuel, Relic and Weapon, and has 2 agents on the first Court card, the third
player 1.

- Choose Battle, then the arrow planet: the battle popup offers the second player as defender and
  dice up to 4 (raid dice allowed, since a defending city is there). Roll.
- Assign the hits the panel lists; destroying the city Outrages the attacker and asks for a Ransack
  of the first Court card, whose agents become Trophies.
- With keys left and a ship left, raid the second player's resources; Stop raiding ends the battle.

## Mid-chapter

The first player led Construction 4. Ships, cities and starports of all three players across three
clusters (one starport damaged, ships damaged at two systems), resources in every player's slots, a
Trophy and a Captive, Tycoon declared, and agents on three Court cards.

- Look over the board, the ambitions' standings and the player panels.
- Build: city or starport where you have pieces, or a ship at your starport.

## Card abilities

The first player led Aggression 4. They hold Gatekeepers, Relic Fence and Prison Wardens, and the
lore Galactic Rifles and Force Beams; their slots hold Fuel, Material and Weapon, and they have a
Captive of the second player's. In the first cluster in play they have ships and a city at the
arrow planet, ships at the gate beside the second player's ship, and a starport at the crescent
planet with 2 ships, where the second player has a fresh and a damaged ship.

- Open Card abilities: each card lists its choices, and only those the rules allow now.
- Pressgang (Prison Wardens) is paid with Material, which frees a slot, so returning the Captive
  for any resource needs no discard. A choice that only fits by discarding shows its discards.
- Guide (Force Beams), crescent planet to its gate: choose how many of each player's fresh and
  damaged ships go, then Guide. The History tells what moved.

## Chapter end

Chapter 2's last round: the first player has led Aggression 5 and holds no more cards; the other two
hold one card each. Tycoon and Tyrant are declared. The first player has the most Material and Fuel,
the second player holds a Captive.

- End the turn, then let each other player Copy with their card and end their turn: the chapter
  ends, Tycoon and Tyrant are scored, and chapter 3 begins with new hands.
- The action panel and the History tell how the chapter scored; the History's table shows chapter
  2's Power from ambitions and everyone's total.

## Last round

The same position in chapter 5, the last.

- Play the round out the same way: the game ends, with the first player winning a tie on Power,
  broken from the initiative. The action panel names the winner and shows the chapter's scoring.

## Catapult

The first player led Mobilization 4 and holds Sprinter Drives. They have a starport with 4 fresh
ships and a damaged one at the first cluster's arrow planet; the second cluster's crescent planet has
the third player's city.

- Choose Move, the arrow planet, then its gate, the next gate and that cluster's arrow planet: the
  action panel offers the ships to move and how many to leave at each gate passed through.
- Leave a fresh ship at the first gate and move: it stays there, and the rest arrive.
- Instead choose Move, then sprint: pick where the fresh ships stopped, then a neighbouring system,
  and Sprint: they move once more, and the History tells both moves.
